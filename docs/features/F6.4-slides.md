## 1. Purpose

A 5-minute talk, as requested: Introduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Working Model, Conclusion (2026-10-20) and Future scope (added 2026-10-28).

## 2. What was built

| Slide | Content | Speaker time |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 Introduction | Title, the problem, the four research questions; data source and licences | ~45 s |
| 2 Literature review | Gleiser & Danon 2003, Park et al. 2007, Bryan & Wang 2011; "our step"; method references | ~45 s |
| 3 Methodology | Pipeline (dump → PySpark → Neo4j + GDS → reports) with sizes; graph model and filters; one metric per RQ | ~60 s |
| 4 Working model | A 16:9 frame for the demo video, plus the list of what it shows | ~75 s |
| 5 Conclusion | One headline number per RQ; the architecture finding; limits | ~45 s |
| 6 Future scope | Petabyte scale: storage (object store, Parquet in Iceberg/Delta, partitioned), processing (Spark on an autoscaling cluster, incremental loads from MusicBrainz replication packets), graph analytics (distributed or multi-GPU, approximate algorithms; Neo4j for serving subgraphs) | ~30 s |

Each slide has speaker notes written for its time. That's about 5 min in total.

## 3. How it works

- **A PowerPoint file** (16:9, 13.33 × 7.5 in), `report/musin-slides.pptx`, built with pptxgenjs. It has a theme (navy, white, blue and orange accents, Calibri) and three layouts: title dark, content light and content dark. Each slide is in its own section, with speaker notes. The deck was first drafted as a Slides artifact; the PPTX replaced it so the deck lives in the repository (2026-10-20).
- **Numbers come from the report tables only** (RULES §9):
  - `data_funnel`: 40.5 M recordings, 3.0 M artists, 1.14 M artists and 3.08 M edges;
  - `rq1_bridges_summary`: 75.8 %;
  - `rq2_communities_runs`: AMI 0.186, modularity 0.868;
  - `rq3_trends`: 16.0 % → 28.4 % and 23.1 %;
  - `rq4_agreement`: Jaccard 0.047;
  - `bench_scaling`: 385 s;
  - `bench_pagerank`: 123×;
  - `communities_runs.csv`: 22 % of artists labelled.
- **Working-model slide:** a live demo was chosen (the frame reads "See the live demo"); the queries and their order are in F6.2 §6.
- **Future scope slide** (2026-10-28): added inside the hand-edited file (cloned from the literature slide's shapes with python-pptx), not by regenerating it, so the manual edits stayed. It is in its own section, and the closing "Thank you" moved to its notes. The slide frames petabyte scale as future work; PRD §15 keeps clusters and HDFS out of this project's scope.
- **Look:** navy and white with the report figures' blue plus orange; Calibri throughout, because it ships with Office and renders the same everywhere; no stock images. The title and demo slides are dark.

## 7. Verification and results

- **Every number** on the slides was checked against its table. One wording was corrected: "full ETL in 385 s" became "builds the full graph from credits in 385 s", since F5.1 times the recording chain, not raw ingest.
- **Literature:** the three papers were confirmed to exist by web search (2026-10-20); full references are in the report.
- **File checks:** the OOXML validator passed. Each slide was rendered through PowerPoint (COM export to PNG) and checked by eye for overflow, overlap and alignment. Two fixes followed: titles left-aligned and the literature cards' text enlarged to fill them.
