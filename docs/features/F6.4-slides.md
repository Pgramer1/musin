## 1. Purpose

A 5-minute talk with five slides, as the team asked (member A, 2026-10-20): Introduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Working Model (video) and Conclusion.

## 2. What was built

| Slide | Content | Speaker time |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 Introduction | Title, the problem, the four research questions; data source and licences | ~45 s |
| 2 Literature review | Gleiser & Danon 2003, Park et al. 2007, Bryan & Wang 2011; "our step"; method references | ~45 s |
| 3 Methodology | Pipeline (dump → PySpark → Neo4j + GDS → reports) with sizes; graph model and filters; one metric per RQ | ~60 s |
| 4 Working model | A 16:9 frame for the demo video, plus the list of what it shows | ~75 s |
| 5 Conclusion | One headline number per RQ; the architecture finding; limits | ~45 s |

Each slide has speaker notes written for its time. That's about 4.5 min in total, leaving room for questions.

## 3. How it works

- **Built as a Slides artifact** (16:9, 1920×1080), so it can be presented in the browser or downloaded as PPTX or PDF. The source (`deck.json` plus one HTML file per slide) is kept in `report/slides/` for the build log.
- **Numbers come from the report tables only** (RULES §9):
  - `data_funnel`: 40.5 M recordings, 3.0 M artists, 1.14 M artists and 3.08 M edges;
  - `rq1_bridges_summary`: 75.8 %;
  - `rq2_communities_runs`: AMI 0.186, modularity 0.868;
  - `rq3_trends`: 16.0 % → 28.4 % and 23.1 %;
  - `rq4_agreement`: Jaccard 0.047;
  - `bench_scaling`: 385 s;
  - `bench_pagerank`: 123×;
  - `communities_runs.csv`: 22 % of artists labelled.
- **Video slide:** a dashed 16:9 frame, sized 1040×585. In the deck editor, select it and use **Image or video** to insert the recording (mp4/webm, at most 20 MB). The shot list is in F6.2 §6.
- **Look:** navy, off-white and two accents (the same blue as the report figures, plus orange); Space Grotesk for headings and IBM Plex Sans for text; no stock images. The demo slide is the dark statement slide.
