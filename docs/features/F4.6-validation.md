## 1. Purpose

A bridge ranking is only convincing if artists that people *know* to be cross-genre collaborators rank well, and if its top entries are real musicians rather than data quirks. PRD §5.6 asks for two checks:

- **A pre-registered list** of ~15 well-known cross-genre artists, fixed before any RQ1 result exists. The report states where they rank.
- **A spot-check of the top 10 of every ranking** for data artefacts.

F4.6 produces the tables for both, and this doc records the outcome.

## 2. What was built

- **`docs/validation-list.md` + `config/validation_artists.csv`** (key `validation.artists_file`). 15 artists with MBIDs and a one-line reason each, committed on 2026-10-12 (`90675e1`) **before any F4.3 code existed**. Chosen by Claude Code at member B's request; the deviations from §5.6 are recorded in [D-037](../decisions.md).
- **`python -m musin.analytics.validation`**: reads every P4 score from the loaded graph's `:Artist` nodes and ranks each measure over the artists where it is defined. Ties are broken by `artistId`, as in F4.1/F4.3.
  - `validation_ranks.csv`: each listed artist's value, rank and percentile on all five measures, plus its community.
  - `validation_summary.csv`: how many listed artists reach the top 10/25/100/1000, and the median rank and percentile.
  - `validation_top10.csv`: the top 10 of every ranking, with type, genre and recording count, for the manual spot-check.
- **4 unit tests:** ranking over defined values with tie-breaks, the top-k summary, the committed list (15 unique, well-formed MBIDs), and that the Cypher returns every measure.

## 3. How it works

1. Read the list. Duplicate MBIDs are an error.
2. One Cypher query returns every artist with `degreeWeighted`, `pagerank`, `betweenness` (F4.1), `genreDiversity`, `bridgeScore` (F4.3) and `louvain` (F4.2). If any measure is missing everywhere, the stage fails and names the stage to run.
3. For each measure, rank the artists where it is defined (1 = highest, ties by `artistId`). Percentile = share of defined artists ranked at or below.
4. Join the list by MBID, summarise, and export the top 10 of each ranking.

**Why ranks over the defined set:** bridge score and diversity exist only for artists with ≥ 5 labelled neighbours (PRD §5). Ranking them among all 1.18 M artists would mostly measure who has enough tagged collaborators.

**The spot-check is manual by design.** Deciding that "Putumayo" is a record label and not a musician needs knowledge the dump doesn't encode. The stage provides the evidence (type, genre, recording count); the verdicts below are written by hand.

**Reading:**

- **Betweenness recognises the list almost perfectly.** 14 of 15 listed artists are in the top 1,000 of 1.18 M (median rank 88). Structurally, these artists do sit between parts of the graph.
- **The bridge score recognises about half the list.** 12 of 15 are above the 89th percentile, and 3 are in the top 100 (Ry Cooder #13, Mark Ronson #34, Santana #54). The median listed artist is at the 96th percentile.
- **The three misses share one cause.** Quincy Jones, Herbie Hancock and Yo‐Yo Ma have *low diversity* (0.26–0.35) despite top-50 betweenness. Their collaboration *weight* is dominated by one large home genre (jazz, classical): hundreds of recordings with jazz musicians outweigh a handful with pop or hip hop stars. PRD §5's diversity is weight-based by design, so it measures where most of the work happens, not whether genre-crossing collaborations exist at all. The report must state this as a limitation of RQ1, not explain it away.
- The bridge score adds little to diversity for these artists. Their betweenness percentiles are all ≥ 0.99, consistent with D-038.

## 8. Known issues and limitations

- **One chooser, not two independent lists** (D-037). The list protects against choosing examples after seeing results, but not against one person's taste.
- **The spot-check verdicts are judgement**, made by Claude Code from names, types and counts. Two entries are unverified. Both members should confirm before the report.
- **Provisional graph.** G1 will likely remove classical and spoken word, which changes several top-10 lists. Re-run after G1 and redo the spot-check.
- **Weight-based diversity under-rates artists with a large home genre** (§7.1). This is a property of the PRD metric. A count-based variant (share of *neighbours* by genre) would rank Quincy Jones differently, but it isn't in the PRD and would need a decision.
