About 75 seconds: play the video. The demo runs eight saved queries in the Neo4j Browser on the full graph. First the overview: 1.1 million artists and four kinds of collaboration. Then the top genre bridges; the first one, Putumayo, is a record label credited as an artist, a data artefact we discuss in the report. Then Ry Cooder, rock by tag, whose partners come from 11 genres: blues, jazz, country, latin and more; and his ego network, coloured by genre. Then degrees of separation: Frank Sinatra to Snoop Dogg in two hops, via Stevie Wonder. Then the three centrality measures, whose top tens barely overlap. Finally the largest community: hip hop with electronic producers, a scene rather than a genre.
