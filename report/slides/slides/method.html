About 60 seconds. The pipeline has four stages. We download the full MusicBrainz export: 40.5 million recordings and 3 million artists. PySpark turns the headerless database dump into typed Parquet, cleans it, and builds the graph in layers: raw, clean, graph. Neo4j with the Graph Data Science library holds the final graph of 1.14 million artists and 3.08 million edges and runs the algorithms; every figure and table in the report is generated by code. The graph has four edge types, from billing credits and from producer and performer credits; the weight is the number of shared recordings. We filter placeholder artists like Various Artists, credits with more than ten artists, groups linked to their own members, and Character and classical artists, which we analyse separately as sensitivity graphs. For each question we defined a metric in advance: for bridges, genre diversity times betweenness percentile; for communities, Louvain compared with genre by NMI and AMI; for trends, the cross-genre and featuring shares per decade; for centrality, three measures and their agreement. We validated against 15 artists we listed before looking at any result, and benchmarked Spark scaling and PageRank in three engines.
