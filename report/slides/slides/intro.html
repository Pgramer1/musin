About 45 seconds. Genres are usually studied one artist at a time, but music is made together: rappers feature singers, producers work across scenes, session players appear on country and jazz records. A collaboration graph shows who connects scenes that would otherwise be separate. MusicBrainz is the largest open music database and records exactly that: who is billed, who is featured, who produced or played. But it is relational, large and noisy, so turning it into a graph we can trust was half the work. We asked four questions: which artists bridge genres, whether collaboration communities match genres, whether cross-genre collaboration has grown over the decades, and who is most central and whether the measures agree. And we processed the complete export, not a sample.
