## 5. Methods

*Owner: B. Sources: PRD §5, §5.5, §5.6; D-033, D-034, D-036, D-037, D-038.*

Say:
- The genre attribution rule: primary genre only; labelled vs unlabelled edges.
- RQ1: genre diversity `D_i = 1 − Σ_g p_ig²`, bridge score `B_i = D_i × percentile_rank(betweenness_i)`, the genre × genre matrix.
- RQ2: Louvain (weighted, run of record single-threaded, stability runs), NMI and AMI against primary genre, also inside the largest component (D-034).
- RQ3: cross-genre share and featuring share per decade, from `edges_by_decade`.
- RQ4: weighted degree, PageRank (to convergence, D-033), betweenness (unweighted, sampled, single-threaded, D-033); agreement by Spearman ρ and top-k Jaccard; credit-only projection as a sensitivity check.
- Degrees of separation: unweighted hops over the four types, canonical path among ties (D-036).
- Validation: the pre-registered list committed before any RQ1 result (PRD §5.6, D-037); spot checks of every top 10.
- Sensitivity variants `characters` and `classical` (F4.7, D-035).
- All parameter values: `rq4_runs` (centrality), `rq2_communities_runs` (Louvain), PRD §5.5.

## 9. Limitations

*Owner: both. Required section (RULES §1.4, §9). Draft points below; turn them into prose and keep every one.*

**Data and coverage**
- **Volunteer-documented data.** Results reflect what MusicBrainz editors chose to enter. Contribution credits (producers, performers) are rich for popular music and sparse elsewhere (F1.4, `profile_contributions.csv`).
- **Genre coverage is a minority** of artists, and labelled coverage falls in recent decades (`rq3_trends`, column "Labelled"). This is the main caveat for RQ1–RQ3.
- **Primary genre is fixed per artist,** not per period (RQ3), and attribution uses the primary genre only, not the genre profile.
- **Classical is excluded** from the main graph (D-039) and analysed only as a variant. Yo‐Yo Ma, on the pre-registered list, is therefore not in the main graph.

**Open G1 items, parked by the team** (`docs/g1-open-items.md`; decide whether each is stated as a limitation or resolved before M4)
- **Spoken word is still in the graph** (D-040). Audiobook authors and narrators rank high on weighted degree in both projections (`rq4_centrality_top_*`). A recording-level filter was proposed but is not in scope.
- **Sampled betweenness is not reproducible across re-imports** of the same graph (P4 §7). The likely cause, unsorted export rows changing Neo4j node ids, is not verified. Within one import the results are reproducible (D-033). Only ranks and percentiles are quoted.
- **No log transform of weights** for the weighted measures (g1-scope-gate §2.3); weight outliers (a live archive) stay at the top of weighted degree.
- **D-027/D-028** (`recording_count` definition; one count per recording and pair) are implemented but not yet signed off by both members.

**Method**
- **Record labels credited as artists** (Putumayo, Vineyard Music) are not on the special-artists list and appear in the top lists.
- **Weight-based diversity under-rates artists with a large home genre** (F4.6), and the bridge score is close to the diversity score for every artist with a defined score, because they all have high betweenness percentiles (D-038).
- **Spot-check verdicts** of the top lists are Claude Code's and need both members' review (F4.6).
- **The validation list** was chosen by Claude Code at member B's request, not independently by both members (D-037); it was still committed before any RQ1 result.

**Benchmarks**
- **One machine:** the numbers justify this project's architecture on this laptop, not general claims about the engines (P5 §7). Run-to-run spread is reported as min–max.
